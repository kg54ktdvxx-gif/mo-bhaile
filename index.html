Mo bhaile

A complete record of your home, for iPhone and iPad. “Mo bhaile” is Irish for “my home”.

Photograph what you own and Mo bhaile drafts the record. Keep receipts, warranties and manuals with your belongings, plan the upkeep, scan your rooms for floor plans, and ask questions about your home. Everything stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

The AI features say they’re unavailable

Drafts from photos and the Co-pilot need Apple Intelligence, on a device that supports it, turned on in the Settings app. Everything else works without it: you can always add things yourself.

Using Mo bhaile on more than one device

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on, then turn on iCloud Sync in Mo bhaile’s Settings (the gear on the Home tab) on each device.

Sharing a home

On the Home tab, open the Homes menu and choose Share Home. For each person you invite, choose whether they can make changes or only look. You can change that, or stop sharing, from the same menu.

Scanning rooms

Floor plans need a device with a LiDAR Scanner. Open a room and choose Scan This Room, or scan a whole floor from Rooms.

Keeping a copy

In Settings, under Export: Export All Data makes a copy of everything, which a new device can restore from (Restore from an Export, when you set up); Export Home Binder (PDF) makes a document for an insurer; Export Items as CSV makes a spreadsheet.

Deleting your data

Delete All Data, in Settings, removes every home and all it holds, from this device and, with iCloud Sync on, from your iCloud. It can’t be undone, so export a copy first if you want one.
