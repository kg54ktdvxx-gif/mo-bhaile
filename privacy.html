Mo bhaile privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

In short

Mo bhaile doesn’t collect, keep or share any of your personal data. Your records stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, and the developer never sees them.

What the app stores, and where

Your homes, rooms, belongings, photos, documents, tasks, notes and settings are stored on your device, protected by iOS data protection.

With iCloud Sync on, they’re also kept in your own private iCloud, so your other devices signed in to your Apple Account have them. Serial numbers, notes and other private details are encrypted on your device before they go to iCloud; with Advanced Data Protection on, only your devices can read them.

Photos keep the details your camera stores with them, such as when and where they were taken.

The widget on the same device shows the tasks due and the warranties ending; with the app lock on, it leaves out their names.

Sharing a home

If you share a home, the people you invite see its records on their own devices, through Apple’s iCloud sharing. You choose for each person whether they can make changes or only look, and each record shows who added it.

If you stop sharing the home, or someone leaves it, it goes from their devices. Your own copy stays with you.

On-device AI

Drafts from your photos and the Co-pilot’s answers use Apple’s on-device language model, where your device supports Apple Intelligence. Your photos and words are processed on your device and aren’t sent to the developer or anyone else.

Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight

So that Spotlight and Siri can find your belongings, their names, makers, models and rooms are added to your device’s search index. The text of your documents isn’t: version 1.0 added it, and later versions remove it.

If you use Siri or Shortcuts with Mo bhaile, or ask Siri about what’s on its screen, iOS handles the request, as it does for any app, and Siri may use Apple’s Private Cloud Compute servers to answer. Mo bhaile itself sends nothing.

To keep Mo bhaile out of Siri, search and suggestions, add it to Excluded Apps in Settings › Siri.

Street View, maps and links

Mo bhaile makes no network requests of its own. If you tap Street View, the home’s address is sent to Apple Maps to find its Look Around view, and nothing from it is kept. Open in Maps passes the address to the maps app you choose.

Links you tap, such as Find the Manual Online or a tradesperson’s website, open in Safari, where that website’s own policy applies.

Camera, photos, contacts and calendar

The camera is used only while you photograph or scan. The photo picker, the contact picker and Add to Calendar are iOS’s own: the app receives only the photos and the contact you choose, and can’t read your photo library, contacts or calendar.

If you turn on the app lock, Face ID is handled entirely by iOS, and the app never receives your face data. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

No accounts, analytics or advertising

There’s no account to create, and the app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code. There’s no server, so your data never reaches the developer.

How long it’s kept, and deleting it

Your records are kept until you delete them. You can delete any record, or a whole home. Delete All Data, in Settings, removes every home and all it holds from your device and, with iCloud Sync on, from your iCloud; export a copy first if you want one.

Deleting the app removes what it stored on your device. What’s in your iCloud stays until you delete it in the app, or remove Mo bhaile’s data from your iCloud storage in the Settings app. To leave a home someone shared with you, use the Homes menu.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on the Mo bhaile website, with a new date.
